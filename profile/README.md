# Hacker Con Badges

**The hardware, firmware and stories behind hacker conference badges worldwide.**

[Explore the catalogue](https://badge.gallery/badges) · [Browse by country](https://badge.gallery/countries) · [Follow the timeline](https://badge.gallery/timeline) · [How we document](https://badge.gallery/methodology)

Hacker badges carry a piece of their event with them: a radio experiment, a tiny synthesizer, a puzzle, a game, or a platform people keep building on long after the conference ends.

[badge.gallery](https://badge.gallery) documents those artifacts and the people who made them. The catalogue connects hardware specifications, firmware, app stores, add-ons, repair notes, event history and original sources. It also records non-electronic identity artifacts when that is what the evidence supports.

## Keeping badges usable

This organization is the home for our developing firmware preservation and restoration effort. We are starting with repository discovery and license review. Mirrors, reproducible builds and hardware-tested restorations will be published as they become available.

Our approach:

- **Preserve the originals.** Retain upstream history, releases, provenance and author credits.
- **Make builds repeatable.** Record toolchains, dependencies, source revisions and build results.
- **Document what works.** Keep successful builds and tests on physical hardware distinct, including board revisions and known limitations.
- **Maintain a clear history.** Identify community repairs separately from original releases and contribute useful fixes upstream where possible.

## Sources and people first

We prefer official documentation, badge-team repositories, event archives and attributable first-hand accounts. Uncertainty stays visible. Images need a documented reuse license or permission, attribution and processing provenance. Worldwide coverage is an ongoing effort.

Have an original firmware release, a missing repository, a correction, or a badge you can help test? [Open an issue](https://github.com/badge-gallery/.github/issues/new) with the badge name, event edition and source links. For photos or other files, include the license or permission basis.
